# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Syllog contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas and evidence
rather than people, welcome differing experience levels, protect private and
security-sensitive information, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats,
or unwanted sexual attention.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove participants. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and Rust community norms,
adapted for the project’s current single-maintainer bootstrap stage.
